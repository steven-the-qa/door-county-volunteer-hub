About Privacy

Privacy

Short version: we collect the least we can, and we only use it to connect you with a nonprofit.

What we collect

When you submit the interest form, we collect your first name, last name, email address, and which opportunity you're interested in. We don't ask for anything else, and there are no accounts.

How it's handled

  • The form is processed by FormSubmit, which relays your message to us by email. FormSubmit does not store submissions.
  • Your message lands in a shared inbox monitored by Sam and Steven.
  • We share your name and email with the specific nonprofit for the opportunity you chose, so they can follow up. We don't share it with anyone else.
  • We don't sell or rent your information, ever.

Retention

We keep your inquiry in our inbox while we help place you and for a short period afterward (about six months), then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Analytics & cookies

This site sets no cookies and runs no third-party advertising or tracking. If we add basic traffic analytics later, it will be a privacy-friendly, cookieless tool and we'll note it here.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: cosmicbobsleigh@gmail.com.
